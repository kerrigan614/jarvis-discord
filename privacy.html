How Discord data is handled

Privacy policy

Effective and last updated: November 25, 2026

1. Scope and operator

This policy applies to J.A.R.V.I.S., a private, self-hosted Discord bot maintained by its individual operator. It operates in the official MARVEL Strike Force Discord server and the operator's private MARVEL Strike Force alliance server. It is not publicly installable.

J.A.R.V.I.S. uses Red-DiscordBot and selected community extensions to provide configured server functionality. This policy describes that bot instance. Discord and external services have their own privacy policies.

2. Data accessed and collected

The bot processes data made available through Discord only as needed for its configured features and within its permissions. Depending on the feature, this can include:

  • Identifiers and member information: Discord user, server, channel, message, and role IDs; usernames, display names, nicknames, join timestamps, roles, permissions, and available membership or role-change information.
  • Messages and interactions: ordinary message content and metadata available to the bot, including embeds or attachment information where relevant; prefix- and mention-command text, DMs, slash-command parameters, button and dropdown selections, and reactions used for configured features.
  • Voice participation: voice-channel state and participation duration maintained by the leveling module. Voice XP is currently disabled. This does not involve recording voice audio.
  • Feature and moderation records: leveling and counting statistics, giveaway entries and results, role-restoration state and relevant role-change audit information, warnings, mutes, moderation actions, historical submitted reports, and related configuration or diagnostic records.

Current Discord access: Members and Message Content intents were restored and enabled on November 25, 2026. The bot can receive member events, maintain member information in its cache, and process ordinary message content in channels it can access, as needed for configured features. Presence intent remains disabled; the bot does not receive privileged presence updates about members' online status or activities. This policy also covers retained records from earlier operation and the recovery modes described below.

3. How data is used

Data is used to provide moderation, member and role management, message and channel automation, leveling, notifications, and administrative functionality. Commands accept the - prefix in server channels and DMs. Server commands also accept a bot mention followed by the command. Slash commands and configured buttons, reactions, and dropdowns provide other interactions.

Counting processes ordinary numeric messages in the configured channel or the number supplied through the optional /count command. Correct ordinary-number posts remain visible; accepted slash-command counts are posted publicly with participant attribution. Invalid ordinary posts are handled under configured deletion and response rules; rejected slash attempts receive a private interaction response. Both methods maintain shared sequence, turn, goal, and contribution records. Private interaction responses do not replace saved records or public count posts.

LevelUp evaluates eligible message content using configured text-length and repetition/similarity checks, as well as cooldown, channel, and role restrictions. Leaderboards use saved participant statistics. Current server leaderboards filter against cached members; historical results and saved records can include people who have left. Weekly award processing verifies current membership separately.

Member join, leave, and role-update events support configured member-change logging, welcome DMs, and sticky-role tracking and restoration, including selected moderation restrictions. Welcome DMs no longer depend on native Discord join notices. The total-member counter uses normal member information and queued updates. The separate RoleNotify extension remains unloaded.

Giveaway buttons record entries and configured entry weights. Entrant names and host attribution use available member information, temporary name caches, and individual account lookups. These display lookups do not establish current membership or change the entry pool. Selected winners are checked separately, and their IDs are retained for staff review if the lookup fails.

Configured message edit and deletion logging can preserve cached pre-edit text or deleted-message content in private moderation records. Messages not received or cached, including text unavailable during restricted operation, may be missing from those logs and cannot be recovered from a deletion event alone. Cleanup transcripts retain the content and metadata available from messages removed by configured timed cleanup. Access to these moderation records is restricted to authorized staff. Message filtering in the official server is handled by Discord's AutoMod rules.

Recovery modes: if Members access is unavailable, installed fallbacks can use native join notices for welcome DMs, relevant role-change audit information and paced individual member lookups for verified sticky-role restoration, and approximate aggregate counts for the member counter. If Message Content access is unavailable, the configured LevelUp activity fallback uses metadata without text comparisons; Counting removes ordinary posts and may DM slash-command instructions at most once per member per server every ten minutes. These recovery paths are inactive while their corresponding intents are enabled. Records retained from recovery operation remain subject to this policy.

J.A.R.V.I.S. is not monetized. Discord data is not sold, used for advertising or marketing profiles, or used to train machine learning or artificial intelligence models.

4. Data stored outside Discord

J.A.R.V.I.S. runs on a Windows Server hosted on an AWS EC2 instance. Its active Amazon EBS volume is encrypted at rest. Limited Discord-derived data is stored on the system running the bot when persistence is needed. This storage is outside Discord. Established examples include:

  • LevelUp: Discord IDs, XP, levels, message counts, voice participation time, and configured weekly progression and reward records.
  • Counting: Discord user IDs, accepted-count and contribution records, last-count timestamps, and shared sequence and goal state. This community does not display a counting leaderboard.
  • RoleTools: user and role IDs, relevant role-change audit entry IDs, verification and restoration state, and the last observed join timestamp for members with verified sticky-role records. These records support selected moderation or notification roles. User IDs, lookup failures, and restoration outcomes may also appear in bot logs.
  • Giveaways: server, channel, message, host, entrant, and selected-winner IDs; entries and their configured weights; prize details and giveaway settings.
  • Warning records: member and issuing moderator IDs, warning points, and warning reasons, including custom text supplied by moderators.
  • Red core and other moderation functionality: a limited username or nickname history, account-linked actions, moderation records, and content deliberately supplied to features such as member reports.

Temporary names and reminder state: the leaderboard and giveaway display features each keep a process-memory name cache limited to 2,000 entries, containing user/server IDs, available names, and lookup timing information. Successful labels normally become eligible for refresh after six hours; unsuccessful lookups have shorter retry intervals. These are freshness and retry intervals, not guaranteed deletion deadlines. Cached labels can survive a cog reload and may remain until refreshed, evicted by the size limit, or cleared by a process restart. Counting also maintains temporary per-member/server timing state to limit instructional DMs.

Text-comparison cache: the configured LevelUp text-checking logic can retain up to five accepted normalized message texts per member per server in memory. With Message Content access restored, these text checks are active again. This cache is separate from saved profile statistics, is limited by entry count rather than a fixed deletion time, and is cleared when the cog or process restarts. It is not populated by the metadata-only activity fallback if text access becomes unavailable. If debug logging is enabled during text-based checks, diagnostic logs may contain short normalized message excerpts, including up to 50 characters in the repetitive-text check. Other features may maintain configuration or records necessary for their operation.

Specific message content may be retained in submitted reports, private message edit and deletion logs, and HTML transcripts of messages removed from configured channels. Logs and transcript attachments are posted within Discord in the private #server-logs channel. Authorized staff may download transcript attachments for moderation review. Downloaded copies are stored outside Discord and may remain after the original messages or attachments are deleted.

J.A.R.V.I.S. does not maintain a general archive of ordinary server conversations. Its configured AutoDelete transcript path builds HTML in memory and uploads it to Discord; it does not save a separate HTML transcript file on the bot's server. Specific content may still remain in moderation records, diagnostic excerpts, the bounded leveling comparison cache, or separately downloaded transcript copies.

Local recovery backups can contain copies of saved configuration and feature records, including leveling, counting, giveaway, and role-restoration data. These are separate from downloaded transcript copies. Changing or deleting a live record does not automatically rewrite an older backup.

5. Access and sharing

The operator administers the bot's stored data. Access to #server-logs, its current or historical message logs, and its cleanup transcripts is restricted to moderators and administrators. Authorized staff may see reports, warning records, entrant lists, and other administrative outputs according to server permissions. Community features such as XP leaderboards, accepted counts, goal announcements, and giveaway host/winner messages may display names, user IDs, and relevant results in configured channels.

Data is transmitted to Discord as needed to provide bot responses and server functionality. External feed and notification features retrieve content from their respective services; they are not used to export ordinary Discord conversations.

Discord API data is not otherwise shared with third parties except with service providers necessary to operate the bot, when expressly directed by the user, or as required by applicable law, consistent with Discord's terms.

6. Retention

Stored data is retained only while necessary for the feature that uses it. Retention depends on the record and configured functionality; there is no universal deletion period of 30 days or less. Some feature and moderation records may be retained for more than 30 days while needed. For example, selected role-restoration data may remain after a member leaves so that configured roles or restrictions can be restored if they return. Retention also applies to message logs, transcript attachments, recovery backups, and separately downloaded transcript copies. Cache refresh intervals described above do not establish an automatic erasure schedule.

Data will be promptly deleted when no longer needed, when the bot stops operating, or following a deletion request from the applicable user or Discord, except where applicable law requires retention. Automated deletion tools may not remove every record; the operator handles remaining records through the manual request process below.

7. Privacy requests and deletion

For questions, correction requests, or deletion requests, contact the operator through Discord. You can send J.A.R.V.I.S. a direct message using -contact followed by your request. If that command is unavailable or you cannot reach the bot, contact an administrator of a server where it operates privately and ask to be connected with the J.A.R.V.I.S. operator.

Include your Discord user ID and the server or feature involved so the operator can locate the relevant records. Requests may require verification that you control the account. Do not post private information in public channels or GitHub issues.

Red provides these commands using J.A.R.V.I.S.'s - prefix in DMs or the official MARVEL Strike Force server's #access-terminal channel. In that server channel, an actual bot mention followed by the command also works, for example @J.A.R.V.I.S. mydata whatdata. Type @ and select the bot from Discord's suggestions:

  • -mydata whatdata explains what types of data Red stores and why.
  • -mydata 3rdparty provides data statements from loaded community extensions.
  • -mydata forgetme initiates deletion of data supported by Red and participating extensions, subject to the bot's confirmation process.

The installed Counting and LevelUp versions do not implement removal of their saved user records through Red's deletion hook. Completing -mydata forgetme should not be treated as confirmation that their contribution, profile, or progression records have been removed. These records require a manual request to the operator.

Automated deletion may also leave role-restoration and giveaway records, temporary name caches, other operational or moderation records, data from extensions that do not implement deletion, message logs, transcript attachments, recovery backups, and separately downloaded transcript copies. To request review and deletion of remaining data, DM the bot with -contact followed by your request, or contact a server administrator privately if the bot is unavailable.

For manual requests, the operator locates the relevant records using the verified Discord user ID, reviews the affected features and retained recovery copies, removes remaining records through manual maintenance as appropriate under the retention policy, and coordinates with authorized staff who hold downloaded transcript copies. The operator follows up with the requester when that review is complete. The automated command's limitations do not replace this manual process.

There is no individual opt-out from message processing necessary for configured moderation and channel automation while participating in affected channels. Deletion removes stored data; later interactions may generate new records needed for the applicable feature.

8. Documentation website and policy updates

This documentation website contains no added analytics, advertising scripts, or tracking cookies. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process visitor information under GitHub's privacy statement. The public documentation repository contains documentation, not the bot's stored user data.

This policy will be updated when the bot's data practices change. The current version and update date will remain available on this page.
